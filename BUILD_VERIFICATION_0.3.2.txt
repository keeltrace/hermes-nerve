Release authority / safety invariants
-------------------------------------
Verified Experience is advisory. It does not own canonical Kanban/task state.
Reconciliation only emits review-required recommendations and does not edit
profiles/configuration. Automatic experience persistence failures are fail-open
with respect to canonical work supervision. Normal experience retrieval and
admission make no provider calls.
